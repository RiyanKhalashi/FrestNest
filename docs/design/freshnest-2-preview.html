FreshNest · design preview · built from your old project

Your old FreshNest look, made calmer, clearer and ready for four roles

The old project's warm ivory, forest green, mint glow, Lora headings and soft rounded cards stay. The changes below fix readability, remove the parts that looked template-made, and give each role its own accent.

Kept from the old design

  • Colors: ivory background, forest text, mint and foam tints, amber highlights
  • Fonts: Lora for headings, Plus Jakarta Sans for text
  • Shapes: soft rounded cards, pill buttons, soft shadows
  • Sidebar with icons, avatar, and a color per role
  • Dark green summary card and the public home page

Improved

  • Readable buttons: deeper green, white text passes contrast (old green failed)
  • Emoji replaced by the drawn crop icons (and listing photos in the real app)
  • Less blur and glow: one soft background glow, no floating, pulsing or blurred blobs
  • Corners 20px instead of 32px, so tables and forms don't look like bubbles
  • Each role gets its accent on menu, buttons and hero: farmer green, dealer blue, transporter orange, admin forest

Palette and type

Same family as the old repo's Tailwind config; two values are deepened so text and buttons stay readable.
