The six seasons, ষড়ঋতু

FreshNest's season advice would follow the Bangla seasons. The badge and tip change by themselves through the year.

গ্রীষ্মGrishma · summermid-Apr – mid-Jun

Heat: warn open vehicles, travel at night.

বর্ষাBorsha · monsoonmid-Jun – mid-Aug

Rain: tarpaulin, expect delays.

শরৎShorot · autumnmid-Aug – mid-Oct

Clearing skies, late rain showers.

হেমন্তHemanta · late autumnmid-Oct – mid-Dec

Aman rice harvest; busy market.

শীতSheet · wintermid-Dec – mid-Feb

Cost-saving on normal vehicles; fog.

বসন্তBoshonto · springmid-Feb – mid-Apr

Mild; mustard and boro rice.

What stays exactly the same

  • The premium layout, fonts, colours, menus and every feature.
  • English everywhere; Bangla only for the season names, always with English next to them.
  • Truck art only in the transporter's screens; farmers, dealers and admins don't get it.
