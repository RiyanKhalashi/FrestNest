One platform, four roles

Each role gets its own home, its own colour and only the tools it needs.

Farmers

Sell from the field, at a fair price, without a middleman.

  • List in a minute: product, quantity in kg, mon or ton, harvest date, price or bidding
  • Choose who sees it: your upazila, district or nationwide
  • Answer offers or let the highest bid win by itself
  • Give your pickup code only when the truck is loaded
Start selling

Dealers

Buy fresh lots near you, with a truck already lined up.

  • Browse nearest first, filter by upazila and product
  • Bid, use Buy Now, or make an offer with your quantity and price
  • Compare transport offers by price, vehicle and rating, counter once
  • Confirm delivery with your code when the produce arrives
Find produce

Transporters

Find loads that fit your vehicles, on routes you already drive.

  • Only deals your vehicle can carry, searched by pickup upazila
  • One offer per deal: vehicle, price, pickup and delivery time
  • Accept or reject a dealer's counter-offer in one tap
  • Report a breakdown with a photo; the load is sold nearby
Find loads

Admins

Keep the marketplace safe, fair and earning.

  • Verify dealers, transporters and their vehicles
  • Approve delivery failures so emergency sales can start
  • Handle reports and cancellations with a written reason
  • Run the business: upazila reports, subscriptions, sponsored offers

How it works

From harvest to handover in four steps

1

List

The farmer lists the produce with a price or open bidding, and picks who can see it.

2

Sell

Dealers bid, buy now or send offers. The best deal is agreed in hours, not days.

3

Move

Matching transporters offer a vehicle and price; the dealer chooses or counters once.

4

Hand over

A code at loading, another at delivery. The deal closes only when both match.

Features

Everything a harvest needs. Nothing that slows it down.

Built around one rule: fresh produce can't wait.

Sell your way

Open bidding where the highest bid wins by itself, or your own price with dealer offers and one counter-offer. Add a Buy Now price if you want it gone fast.

Proof at every handover

The farmer gives a 4-digit code at loading, the dealer gives another at delivery. No code, no handover.

The right vehicle, every time

Deals reach only transporters whose vehicle fits the load and the produce. A better vehicle can always offer.

Bargain on transport

Dealers compare offers with rating and vehicle, and can counter once. A yes books the truck straight away.

Season-aware advice

Summer and monsoon warnings for open vehicles, a cost-saving tag in winter. Advice only, never a block.

A breakdown doesn't mean a lost harvest

The driver reports the problem with a photo, an admin approves, and the load goes on emergency sale to dealers near the stuck vehicle, widening step by step until someone buys.
